Privacy

Privacy Policy

Melori is built by Kribu with privacy-first design in mind. Melori is intended to help people manage real-life context such as email, calendar, reminders, planning, notes, and follow-through with clarity and control.

Our Principles

  • We collect the minimum data necessary to deliver Melori's personal assistant experience.
  • We use encryption in transit and at rest wherever possible.
  • We never sell personal data to third parties.
  • We design memory and context features to stay useful, bounded, and under user control.
  • We clearly communicate what data is used and why as Melori evolves.

Data Use

Melori may use information you choose to connect or provide, such as account details, calendar context, email metadata or content needed for assistant features, reminders, notes, preferences, and support messages. This information is used to provide, improve, secure, and support Melori.

Contact

This policy will evolve as Melori moves toward release. For privacy questions, contact help@askmelori.ai.
